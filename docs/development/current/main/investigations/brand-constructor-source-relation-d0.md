# Brand Constructor Source Relation D0

Status: selected design stop
Parent: `brand-program-declaration-catalog-i0.md`
Row: `BRAND-CONSTRUCTOR-SOURCE-RELATION-D0`

## Design brief

Decision: Audit one catalog-backed, source-site Brand constructor/unwrap
relation before selecting an implementation; do not pair resolver misses to
catalog rows by spelling.
Source authority + canonical issuer: The effective Brand catalog plus the
resolver's exact FunctionCall/MethodCall source sites must be co-sealed by one
resolved-semantics issuer before child effects.
Non-authority: Raw `is_brand_declared(name)`, FreeStatic miss, AST/JSON names,
Stage1 BrandConstruct, argument ValueIds, and the compatibility cache cannot
issue constructor or unwrap membership.
Fail-fast boundary: Foreign, duplicate, missing, wrong-arity, or colliding site
relations reject before argument descent; no missing relation falls back to
raw Brand classification.
Smallest next slice: Produce a read-only census of resolver site identities,
catalog lending, collision priority, arity timing, and constructor/unwrap
consumers, then accept exactly one bounded BoxCount or report NoSafeSlice.
Non-claims: No code, new receipt, raw consumer switch, nominal MIR Brand value,
Stage1 unification, cache retirement, runtime/ABI, backend, fallback, or retry.

## Audit acceptance

- Name the exact source site carried through resolver output for natural
  `Brand(value)` and `Brand.unwrap(value)` forms.
- Prove catalog membership and the site relation meet before child traversal.
- Preserve current Brand-first collisions and exactly-one-argument timing, or
  stop for a separate language decision.
- Name the later consumer cutover and the exact raw authority deleted there;
  do not mix that BoxShape retirement into the relation BoxCount.
