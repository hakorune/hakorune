## Execution brief

Decision: Choose one program-wide disposition for relation-less production
admissions before the landed projection may be consumed; do not keep a
compatibility name authority beside the verified relation.
Source authority + canonical issuer: Effective Brand declaration facts plus an
exact semantic owner/site product are the only admissible constructor issuer;
an admission without them must either gain that same authority or be retired
before raw child effects.
Non-authority: `is_brand_declared`, lineage, AST spelling/span, RawLegacy
success, Deferred status, compatibility fixtures, and mutable declaration maps
cannot justify preservation or infer a relation.
Fail-fast boundary: For each blocking admission, prove caller-zero/retirement
or name the exact owner/site issuance before Builder entry; no missing row may
become ordinary or retry the name probe.
Smallest next slice: Select exactly one blocking family—Deferred Script,
callable Compatibility, instance constructor, nested/Main, or RawLegacy—and
close its natural Brand caller census plus issuance/retirement boundary.
Non-claims: No aggregate compatibility receipt, partial raw cutover, unwrap
activation, nominal Brand type/value, runtime, backend, fallback, or broad
special-call registry.

## Exit

The D2 selected `BRAND-INSTANCE-CONSTRUCTOR-SOURCE-RELATION-D0` as the first
bounded BoxCount issuance.  Instance constructors already have a finite
parser-owned source batch and one production lowering edge, so they can prove
the semantic-product shape without widening the deliberately ordinary-callable
catalog.  Nested methods have the checked-in natural callers and follow as a
separate family; Deferred, Compatibility, and RawLegacy remain `NoSafeSlice`.

Global `is_brand_declared` retirement remains forbidden.  The landed
Constructor/NonBrand projection stays unconsumed until every production
admission has an exact disposition or an explicit caller-zero retirement.
