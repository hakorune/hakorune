## Why this lane is active again

The warning cohort through I147 is a measured, clean stopping point: the lib
baseline reached **1,672 warnings** and lib-test remained **543**, with no
suppression and no unclassified red. The remaining dead-code warnings belong
to their semantic owners and are parked for a later batch. Continuing to pick
warning accessors now would postpone the compiler's actual meaning pipeline.

The composite LoopBreak prerequisites already landed: forest/path extension
evidence is **28/28**, composite source projection is **3/3**, and the direct
source physical row remains **15/15**. The missing work is the explicit
source-to-publication chain, not another warning census.
